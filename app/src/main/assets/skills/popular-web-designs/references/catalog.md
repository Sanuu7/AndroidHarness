# Brand crib

Load this with skill_view(name="popular-web-designs", file_path="references/catalog.md").
Each block is enough to fake the brand. Do not mix palettes.

## Stripe
- bg #ffffff / ink #0a2540 / accent #635bff / muted #425466
- font: sohne-like: 'Sora' or 'Inter',  tight hero 600
- radius 6-8px, thin borders #e6ebf1
- do: lots of whitespace, navy type. don't: neon crypto gradients

## Linear
- bg #08090a / panel #141516 / ink #f7f8f8 / accent #5e6ad2
- font: 'Inter' or 'IBM Plex Sans', compact, 13-14px UI
- radius 8px, hairline #23252a
- do: dense, dark, quiet. don't: chunky shadows

## Vercel
- bg #000 / ink #ededed / accent #fff / muted #888
- font: 'Inter', huge black/white contrast,  tight tracking on hero
- radius 8-12, no chrome
- do: one idea per section. don't: color for its own sake

## Notion
- bg #ffffff / ink #37352f / accent #2383e2 / wash #f7f6f3
- font: 'Lora' headlines + 'Inter' body (notion-ish)
- radius 4, paper feel
- do: editorial, warm gray. don't: startup purple

## Airbnb
